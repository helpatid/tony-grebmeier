Some days you know what needs your attention. Other days you need a place to sort through your thoughts. Follow the questions and write what comes to mind. A few words are enough.

What’s on the page

Every page helps you get started.

Every morning

Plan Your Day

Where you are today (personal, family and friends, work), what you’re grateful for, who you’ll reach out to, what matters most, and an hour-by-hour plan from 4 AM to 11 PM.

Every night

Close Your Day

Rate your energy, focus, relationships, attention, progress and follow-through from 1 to 5. Then the best part of the day, what it taught you, and today in your own words.

Sunday

Plan Your Week

What this week is about, the three big things, day by day, and a parking lot for everything else.

Sunday

Close Your Week

The same six ratings, plus wins, losses, learnings and unfinished business.
